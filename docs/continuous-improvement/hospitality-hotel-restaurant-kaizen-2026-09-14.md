# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Added a routed hospitality proposal overlay covering hotel/resort/lodge/inn,
guest-house, restaurant, bar, catering and event-venue bids. It connects guest
outcomes to operating flows, evidence, acceptance, staffing, safety, privacy,
payments, finance, implementation and handover. It explicitly rejects feature
selling without an accountable owner and acceptance oracle.

## Evidence discipline

The supplied books were admitted as durable concept inputs only: front-office
and event operating sequences, restaurant controls, design/flow, leadership,
commercial realism and revenue-management logic. Historical benchmarks and
market/regulatory claims remain context-bound. Current statutory, payment,
food-safety, privacy, accessibility and search claims route to primary sources
and the relevant companion engine.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: proposals with a hospitality operating spine and proof-to-oracle
mapping will be easier to evaluate and safer to hand into delivery. Measure
compliance-matrix completeness, unresolved assumptions, red-team findings and
handoff defects on the next authorised proposal. Rollback by unlinking the
overlay. Next review: 2026-10-14; live win rate and delivery evidence are
`NOT_ASSESSED` until a real engagement is reviewed.
