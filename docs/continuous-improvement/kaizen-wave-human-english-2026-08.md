# Kaizen Wave — Human English for Proposals

Date: 2026-08-27
Scope: proposals, bids, EOIs, cover letters, executive summaries, methodologies, case studies, and evaluator-facing messages.

## Baseline

This language-craft screening scored 58/100; it is not a compliance or submission certification. Strengths were the profile/ToR routes, evidence and envelope controls, premium commercial writing, and anti-slop cadence. Gaps were the absence of a local synthesis of the supplied books and no single method connecting evaluator purpose, genre, grammar, collocation, rhythm, and proof.

## Root cause

The proposal engine had strong evaluator logic but language guidance was spread across profile, premium, language, and anti-slop skills. A user entering through a single section could receive polished prose without the full reader-craft pass.

## Improvement implemented

- Added `book-extractions/human-english-craft-synthesis-2026.md` with source limits, five passes, proposal register, grammar/lexical checks, and original anti-slop examples.
- Routed it through `AGENTS.md`, the parent proposal skill, premium-commercial-writing, and language-standards.
- Preserved profile, ToR, compliance, evidence, finance, and envelope gates; readable prose cannot create proposer credentials or compliance evidence.

## Engine-level experiment and evidence

Experiment: verify that the local reference exists and is linked from the router and two proposal writing gates.
Success criterion: all required files and links resolve without changing submission controls.
Result: PASS — the local reference and intended router/anchor links resolve. Output-quality improvement remains unclaimed until two human reviewers assess representative proposal sections.

## Guardrails and rollback

On failure, remove only the new reference links and keep the previous proposal gates. Never store raw book text or accept a language improvement as proof of capability, compliance, price, or delivery capacity.

## Acceptance and next review

Review one cover letter, one executive summary, one methodology, one case study, and one financial narrative. Require evaluator fit, ToR specificity, correct grammar, natural rhythm, claim-proof alignment, and zero fabricated credentials. Re-audit date: 2026-09-26.
