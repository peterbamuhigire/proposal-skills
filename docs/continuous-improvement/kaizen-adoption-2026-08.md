# Proposal Skills — Kaizen Adoption Plan

Baseline: 59/100 capped in the July 2026 portfolio audit. Target: 95/100; future audits remain capped at 65/100.

## Product scope

Consulting proposals, bids, tenders, EOIs, technical and financial proposals, donor responses, methodologies, M&E plans, change-management plans, AI/software transformation proposals, and bid evidence packs.

## P0 changes

- Add `meta/kaizen-improvement-system` to the proposal router and final release gate.
- Add an evaluator-simulation and product-audit reference with the 65-cap/95-target model.
- Strengthen proposal storytelling with an evidence-led evaluator journey: audience, problem tension, choice, proof, delivery consequence, and decision.
- Strengthen methodology, M&E, change-management, and technical strategy references with PDCA, QC Story, root-cause analysis, standardisation, and learning reviews.

## P1 changes

- Add a sanitised bid fixture showing ToR extraction, compliance matrix, evaluator score, red-team findings, revisions, and final evidence.
- Add *Paid for Your Perspective* patterns for expert positioning, screening, compliance boundaries, preparation, call notes, and durable knowledge products.
- Add Facility Move patterns for implementation governance, readiness, continuity, cutover, stabilisation, and lessons learned.

## Book-to-skill application

XP 2026 supports Agile transformation, AI adoption, leadership/culture, experimentation, UX pilots, and evidence-based process claims. Lean and Applying the Kaizen in Africa support small experiments, 5S/muda, QC Story, participatory learning, and standardisation. Nonprofit strategy supports mission/evidence/stakeholder plans. Designing for AI and Platform Enterprise support credible AI/platform proposals; verify current legal and technology claims independently.

## Acceptance evidence

Run `source_ingestion_guardrail.py`, validators, routing smoke tests, anti-slop and document/render gates, then a blind evaluator review. A proposal is not improved until the review evidence shows the named gap closed.
