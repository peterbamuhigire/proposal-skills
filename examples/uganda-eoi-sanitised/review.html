Organising a fictional records unit

A method-led response specimen with explicit evidence and commercial boundaries.

Fictional Civic Office asks for a practical method to organise a made-up records unit. The response starts with an agreed sample inventory, checks a small batch, and records exceptions before the next batch proceeds.

Response method

The fictional team would agree a sample inventory with the records unit, test a small batch against it, then organise the remaining records. A designated reviewer would compare each batch with the agreed inventory and record exceptions for correction.

This method depends on access to the records, an agreed classification scheme and a named client contact. No past performance or completed assignment is claimed.
