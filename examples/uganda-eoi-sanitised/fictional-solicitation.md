# Fictional request for an expression of interest

**TRAINING SPECIMEN — wholly fictional; not issued by a real public body.**

## Purpose

Fictional Civic Office invites a short expression of interest describing a method for organising paper records for a made-up records unit. This invented scenario is used only to demonstrate traceable response drafting.

## Requirements

| Clause | Mandatory request | Response location |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Describe the proposed delivery method and its quality checks. | Technical methodology |
| 3.2 (added by fictional Addendum A1) | Provide a risk register listing each material risk, its owner, an observable trigger and a response. | Technical methodology and compliance matrix |
| 6.2 | Provide a financial schedule in a separate financial envelope. This synthetic specimen intentionally supplies no amount. | Financial schedule |

## Submission deadline · fictional section 1.4

The training scenario uses 15 January 2030 at 17:00 East Africa Time (UTC+03:00). This is a fictional test value, not an active or authentic submission deadline.

## Submission controls (fictional)

The fictional authorised signatory, technical and financial reviewers, and separate submission owner appear only as synthetic state records in the validator input. No real person, signature, approval or authority is represented.

Technical and financial material are assigned to separate envelopes. This specimen contains no price, rate, currency amount or tax treatment.
