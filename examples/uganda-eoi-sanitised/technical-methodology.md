# Technical response: proposed method

**TRAINING SPECIMEN — no past-performance claim or real supplier is represented.**

## Delivery method

The fictional team would first agree a sample inventory with the records unit, then test a small batch against that inventory before organising the remaining records. A designated reviewer would compare each completed batch with the agreed inventory and record exceptions for correction.

This method is conditional on access to the records, an agreed classification scheme and a named client contact. The specimen contains no claim that the work has been performed.

## Addendum A1: risk register

| Fictional risk | Owner | Observable trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| The sample inventory does not match the records supplied. | Fictional records lead | The reviewer finds an item with no matching inventory entry. | Pause the affected batch; log the exception; agree the inventory update with the fictional client contact before continuing. |
| A batch cannot be checked against the agreed sample. | Fictional quality reviewer | Required sample fields are missing from the batch record. | Hold that batch from acceptance; complete the missing fields and repeat the check. |

These are proposed controls for the invented scenario, not a statement of actual capacity or a guarantee of outcome.
