# Proposal Compliance and QC Story Reference

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md)

Use for technical methodologies, bids, tenders, EOIs, consulting proposals, and implementation plans. This is a synthesis of *Applying the Kaizen in Africa*, *Paid for Your Perspective*, *The Nonprofit Guide to Strategic Planning*, and *Facility Move Playbook*; current procurement and legal requirements still require independent verification.

## Compliance screen

| Check | Evidence/source | Owner | Reviewer/status | Consequence if unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eligibility, authority, conflicts, confidentiality | RFP/ToR, declarations, profile records | Proposal lead | Independent reviewer | Stop submission or qualify |
| Mandatory technical response | Compliance matrix and page/format rule | Section owner | Proposal lead | Block release |
| Evidence and experience claim | Source file, contract, referee, role and outcome | Profile owner | Evidence reviewer | Narrow or remove claim |
| Technical/financial separation | Submission pack and file map | Proposal lead | Bid manager | Risk of disqualification |
| Current external claim | Digital Research source register | Research owner | Named reviewer | Mark not assessed |

## QC Story / PDCA card

Record: problem, scope, baseline, target, evidence, root cause, countermeasure options, selected action, owner, timebox, expected signal, guardrail, result, residual gap, standardisation/training, and next review. Link the card to the ToR criterion, deliverable, indicator, risk, and evidence file.

## Expert perspective boundary

Screen each advisory or expert-network engagement for buyer purpose, subject fit, conflicts, confidentiality, non-public information, contract/compensation restrictions, and whether the requested view is within verified competence. Use informed perspective as contextual evidence, not as authority to provide legal, tax, audit, investment, or regulated advice without the appropriate reviewer.

## Facility transition gate

For implementation or relocation work, require charter, decision rights, current-state baseline, continuity/fallback, cutover/rollback, stabilisation measures, acceptance evidence, closeout reconciliation, and lessons-learned standardisation. Do not claim readiness from activity completion alone.
