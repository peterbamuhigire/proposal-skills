# One-Page Account Plan Template

Parent skill: [Key-Account Pursuit and Account Plan](../SKILL.md).

**When to read:** at workflow step 9, to write or refresh the account plan. Fill every section or mark it `not assessed` with the evidence needed. Keep it to one page for leadership; supporting tables go in an appendix.

## Template

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client's pragmatic strategy | Objectives, market position, decision process, pain points, future vision (one line each, sourced) |
| 2 | Account type and relationship position | Strategic / star / status / streamline; typology now → target, with date |
| 3 | Buying group (GRASP summary) | Decider, controller, key influencers, gatekeeper; relationship state; multi-threading gaps |
| 4 | Customer SWOT moves | The two or three attack / convert / defend / block moves we can support |
| 5 | Value hypotheses | Client metric, baseline, target, measurement method, owner (client and ours) |
| 6 | Expansion path | Account Cube grid: next step, business problem it solves, entry contact |
| 7 | Joint objectives and measures | Including measures the client defines for us |
| 8 | Team and sponsor | Account lead, RASIC summary, sponsor and agreed commitment |
| 9 | Risks and contingencies | Dependency, key-person, procurement, delivery, payment and relationship risks with actions |
| 10 | Relationship health | Latest survey or review result, both views, top gap |
| 11 | Cadence | Contact and review rhythm by level; quarterly value review date |
| 12 | Decisions required from leadership | Investment, people, pricing latitude, with deadline |

**Objective format:** "By [date], move [account] from [position] to [position] by agreeing [n] joint measures, co-investing in [initiative] and establishing a quarterly value review with [client executive]."

**Value line format:** "Based on your [data source], we estimate [solution] will [reduce / increase] [client metric] by about [x], worth roughly UGX [amount] a year, measured against a baseline agreed with your [finance / operations] team within [30] days."

## Worked example (fictional, illustrative figures)

| # | Section | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pragmatic strategy | Published aim: digital-first SME banking. Interviews: branch managers need walk-in lead flow; the marketing director needs board-ready proof of return. Source: 2025 annual report; three interviews, June. |
| 2 | Type and position | Strategic. Now: competitive interaction (annual tender). Target: collaborative partnership within 18 months. |
| 3 | Buying group | Marketing director: decider, values board credibility, warm. Head of digital: influencer and user, wants data, neutral. Procurement: buyer and gatekeeper, wants compliance and price, neutral. Finance controller: controller, wants predictable monthly cost, unknown. Gap: no tie to the executive director. |
| 4 | SWOT moves | Attack: SME account growth in upcountry branches. Convert: weak lead tracking. |
| 5 | Value hypothesis | SME accounts opened per branch per month: baseline to be agreed with the bank's MIS team in 30 days; target +20% in pilot branches by Q2; measured from core-banking reports. |
| 6 | Expansion path | Next: employer-brand content for HR (function step); problem: graduate recruitment; entry via the marketing director. |
| 7 | Joint measures | Cost per qualified SME lead; branch conversion rate; campaign delivery on schedule (client-defined). |
| 8 | Team and sponsor | Account lead plus six-person pod; founder as sponsor, two senior meetings a year, monthly check-in. |
| 9 | Risks | 40% of our revenue from this client (above our own threshold): add two star accounts this year. |
| 10 | Health | Our view: strong on delivery; client view: slow reporting. Action: automate monthly report. |
| 11 | Cadence | Weekly operations call; monthly report; quarterly value review; renewal preparation starts 90 days before expiry. |
| 12 | Decisions | Approve reporting automation budget by 30 July; confirm pricing latitude for renewal options. |

At renewal: open with the value delivered, present equivalent-value options, trade conditional concessions, and hold a short post-settlement review (see [strategic-account negotiation](../../premium-pricing-and-value-defense/references/strategic-account-negotiation.md)).
