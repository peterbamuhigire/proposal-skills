# Premium service proposals: promise, proof and delivery

Parent: [Premium Client Proposal Strategy](../SKILL.md).

## Benchmark and judgement

[Shipley's proposal assessment](https://www.shipleywins.com/consulting/proposal-assessment) covers value proposition, compliance, clarity, persuasion, visual effectiveness and organisation (accessed 2026-09-22). This is an advertised assessment scope, not evidence of a guaranteed win rate. Our operating inference is to make every premium promise defensible to the evaluator and executable by the delivery team.

Start with the actual decision and solicitation. For Uganda public procurement, inspect the applicable current [PPDA document](https://www.ppda.go.ug/standard-bidding-documents/) and the issued solicitation/addenda. The index is a discovery route, not a substitute for the governing document. Do not assert compliance with every local and international standard.

## Promise-to-proof workpaper

For World Bank-funded work, use the [procurement framework and consulting-document routes](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/what-we-do/project-procurement/framework)
(opened 2026-09-22) to locate the applicable documents. Read the actual project
rules and issued RFP; international templates do not override local or
solicitation-specific requirements by assumption.

Complete before writing the executive summary:

| Field | Required decision |
|---|---|
| Buyer problem | What decision, risk or loss has the buyer actually evidenced? |
| Proposed result | What changes, for whom, and by when? Separate target from contractual deliverable. |
| Mechanism | Which activities and dependencies could produce the result? |
| Evidence | Comparable case, sample, demonstrated method or explicitly untested hypothesis; include rights and limits. |
| Capacity | Named role, availability, effort, skill, subcontractor and client obligation. |
| Commercial basis | Fee, media/third-party costs, tax/FX assumptions, payment milestones, exclusions and change control. |
| Acceptance | Observable test, artefact, owner and rejection/remedy procedure. |
| Countercase | Why might a cheaper, internal or AI-assisted alternative be sufficient? |
| Failure plan | What happens if access, data, approvals, capacity or results disappoint? |

Do not equate affluent buyers with a need for exclusivity. Professional-service value may be precision, expertise, reduced risk, speed, ownership or sustained support. State the actual basis for the premium. Avoid unnamed senior experts, invented client logos, inflated CVs and unsubstantiated savings.

## Service-specific scope

| Service | Commitments the supplier can usually define | Outcomes requiring conditional targets and measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Digital marketing | Research, strategy, creative units, campaign setup, approved operation, measurement and review | Incremental demand, contribution, retention and payback |
| Website | Discovery, content, working journeys, scoped QA, training, ownership and support | Search visibility, conversion uplift and revenue |
| Business/marketing plan | Evidence, alternatives, reconciled model, implementation and challenge process | Funding approval, market acceptance and growth |
| Advisory | Analysis, recommendations, workshops and agreed implementation assistance | Decisions or results controlled jointly with the client |

Adapt to the contract; this table is not legal advice. A performance-based fee needs an agreed baseline, attribution method, cost boundary, exclusions, audit rights, timing and dispute procedure. Route the economics through Chwezi and the terms through the appropriate reviewer. Do not sell a guaranteed result with an unmeasurable definition.

## Construct the evaluator's argument

1. Lead with the buyer's specific decision and the recommended scope.
2. State a small number of discriminators. For each: buyer consequence, evidence, delivery mechanism and limitation.
3. Map every requirement to the response location and evidence. Preserve mandatory formats, limits and envelope separation.
4. Show method through concrete work products and decisions, not a generic discover/design/deliver diagram.
5. Reconcile staffing, schedule, costs, dependencies and acceptance. A premium retainer cannot depend on more work than its team can supply.
6. Compare a credible alternative, including doing less. Explain the trade-off without disparaging the buyer or cheaper providers.
7. Describe implementation, adoption, measurement, support and handover. The proposal should survive transfer to the delivery lead.
8. Review against the actual evaluation criteria, challenge the strongest claim and repair failures before document production.

## Fee defence and service recovery

Make options different in scope, risk allocation or service intensity; do not manufacture a decoy. Keep a credible minimum scope. If the budget cannot support it, reduce scope, offer paid discovery or decline. Explain where AI improves efficiency and where the team remains accountable. Price must be supported by cost, capacity and buyer value evidence rather than a claimed prestige multiplier.

Define escalation, response versus resolution, change requests, delayed client inputs, correction of errors and exit/handover. Test whether a delivery owner could execute the proposal without inventing missing work. Record uncertain standards, legal terms and professional reviews as open gates.

## Anti-slop and rejection challenges

- Replace the client name with a competitor's. If the argument still fits, revise the diagnosis and proof.
- Ask the reviewer to find the source of every number and credential. An untraceable one is removed or qualified.
- Remove the words premium, innovative and world-class. The offer should still justify its fee.
- Inspect a deliverable sample and failure path; a methodology heading is not evidence.
- Test scope against the actual effort and cost schedule. Reject an attractive narrative with impossible capacity.

Synthetic rejection case: a social retainer promises guaranteed sales, counts views as qualified leads and excludes the cost of media from its ROI. Reject the commitment; define supplier-controlled work, qualified-opportunity criteria, full cost and a conditional measurement plan. No fee option may conceal the correction.

## Release record

Retain requirement trace, claim-proof matrix, capacity/cost reconciliation, current-source decisions, risk/remedy schedule, document/render review and reviewer disposition. Run the engine's high-stakes bid red-team when applicable. A mechanical validator cannot certify persuasion, compliance or award probability.
