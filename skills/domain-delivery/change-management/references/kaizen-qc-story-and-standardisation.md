# Kaizen QC Story and Proposal Delivery Improvement

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md)

Use when a proposal methodology, implementation plan, M&E system, change programme, or consulting delivery must show how learning becomes standard work.

Structure the improvement story as: problem and scope; baseline; target; evidence and root cause; countermeasure options; selected action and owner; timebox; result; residual gap; standardisation/training; next cycle. Link each step to the ToR, deliverable, indicator, risk, and evidence owner.

For proposals, this is a credibility device, not a decorative Japanese label. Never promise a percentage improvement without a source or client baseline. The method must preserve decision rights, safeguarding, quality, legal compliance, and evaluator-specific requirements.
