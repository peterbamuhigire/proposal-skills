# Proposal and grant specialists

Return to the [proposal router](../SKILL.md) for the owning workflow.

At bid intake, select the roles whose work answers a solicitation requirement. Give each contributor its clause or criterion, evidence, owner, due time and receiving section.

| Specialist | When to involve them | What they should return |
|---|---|---|
| Bid / proposal manager | On release of the RFP, RFA or EOI and whenever an addendum changes the response. | A clause-by-clause compliance matrix covering eligibility, content, format and page limits. Work back from the submission deadline and time zone to schedule drafting, Pink outline review, Red evaluator review and Gold release review. Deliver a storyboard with win themes, evidence, section owners and page budgets. |
| Capture manager / business development lead | During opportunity development, often three to twelve months before release where the opportunity permits. | A capture plan linking buyer priorities, competitors and evidenced differentiators. Recommend prime, subcontractor, consortium or joint venture roles. Give a price-to-win range with sources, assumptions and confidence. Use authorised market engagement and permitted buyer information. |
| Technical / subject matter expert (SME) | After the scope and delivery constraints are understood, before the schedule and price are fixed. | A methodology or programme design, work breakdown and implementation timeline. Supply domain evidence for proposed innovations, such as a relevant architecture or field protocol, and a risk and quality plan with owners and acceptance checks. |
| Grant / proposal writer | Throughout drafting, once the team can identify the criteria, technical inputs and supporting records. | One coherent narrative mapped to the rubric, an executive summary and cover letter aligned to the buyer or donor mission, and past-performance cases with attributable outcomes. Quantify results where the named firm's or partner's records support them. |
| Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning (MEAL) specialist | During programme design for a donor or results-based bid, before budgeting data collection. | A theory of change and logframe connecting activities to outputs, outcomes and impact. A performance monitoring plan defines indicators, baselines, targets, data sources, collection methods, owners and dates. Set feedback channels, learning reviews, adaptation decisions and donor reporting dates. Flag baselines awaiting collection. |
| Pricing / cost proposal specialist | In parallel with technical drafting and again after changes to staffing, scope or schedule. | A line-item cost volume reconciling level of effort, labour rates, fringe, direct expenses and the applicable negotiated indirect rate or overhead basis. Explain reasonableness, allowability and allocation against the call. Show cost share, caps, sensitivities and comparison with the price-to-win estimate. |
| Contracts, legal and compliance officer | At kickoff, during teaming negotiations and before sign-off. | A terms risk review and proposed redlines or exceptions for IP, liability, payment and regulatory clauses. Prepare NDAs, teaming agreements, MOUs and subcontract documents for authorised signatories. Track executed copies and required representations, tax or audit clearances and anti-corruption declarations, with issuer, validity and outstanding items. |
| Human resources / key personnel recruiter | When the solicitation names key posts, qualifications or availability requirements. | Evidence-backed CVs or bios in the prescribed format, a staffing chart showing reporting lines, level of effort and surge cover, and a register of signed availability, exclusivity or commitment letters. Identify unconfirmed personnel before their hours enter the offer. |
| Proposal editor / red-team reviewer | When an assembled draft can be scored, often around 70% and 90% completion if the schedule allows. | An independent evaluator scorecard using the published criteria and weights, with evidence locators, gaps and repair owners. Edit jargon, contradictions and repetition. Cross-check every mandatory clause and addendum against the final version and record unresolved exceptions. |
| Graphic designer / desktop publisher (DTP) | Once narrative and data are stable enough for layout, with time for corrections after rendering. | Diagrams with action captions explaining the method, governance, results chain or geography. Apply the prescribed fonts, margins, templates and page limits. Deliver rendered files in the requested envelope structure, with index, bookmarks, links and size optimisation as permitted. Inspect pagination, legibility and file sizes before handoff for authorised upload. |

When an addendum changes the delivery period, the bid manager identifies affected clauses, the SME revises the work plan, staffing confirms availability and pricing reconciles the new level of effort. The reviewer checks the final technical and financial volumes against the same addendum. Keep draft, signed, verified and submitted states distinct.
