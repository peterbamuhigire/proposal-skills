# Editorial specialists

Use the [proposal router](../SKILL.md) to assign editorial work to a bid section. The bid manager owns compliance and the named signatory owns release. Recheck the final proof against page limits and envelope rules after an edit.

| Specialist | When to involve them | What they should return |
|---|---|---|
| Commissioning / acquisitions editor | Before drafting, when deciding whether the work fits a readership, market or institution. | An editorial brief naming the reader, purpose, comparable titles and positioning. Agree the section or chapter outline, word budget, delivery dates, house style, rights parameters and publication milestones. |
| Subject-matter researcher / fact-checker | During research and again after substantive revisions, before publication. | An annotated dossier of primary and relevant secondary sources, archival records and authorised interviews. Verify dates, figures, quotations and technical claims in a ledger with locators, corrections and unresolved items. Record attribution and clearance for reused text, data and images. |
| Lead author / ghostwriter | Once the brief, evidence and intended voice support drafting. | A complete first manuscript with a sustained thesis or narrative, chapter summaries and transitions. Return author queries for missing evidence, examples, technical input or visuals. Keep attributed views and experience with their actual owner. |
| Developmental editor | After the first complete draft, while large structural changes are still economical. | An assessment letter testing the argument, pacing and audience fit, an ordered revision plan for cuts, moves, mergers and expansions, and manuscript annotations locating weak transitions, contradictions or gaps. |
| Line / stylistic editor | After the author accepts the structure. | Tracked sentence and paragraph edits for cadence, clarity, word choice, transitions and consistent register. Preserve deliberate voice and query changes that could alter a technical claim. |
| Copyeditor | After line edits are accepted and before layout. | A mechanically corrected manuscript against the agreed house or citation style, a project style sheet for names, terms, spelling and capitals, and a check of citations, bibliography, notes, numbering and cross-references. |
| Sensitivity reader / legal and defamation reviewer | During pre-production when representation, privacy, allegations, confidentiality or third-party rights affect publication. | A representation assessment from a reader with relevant context, passage-level legal risk flags for qualified counsel, and suggested sourcing, consent, redaction or wording changes. Record the responsible reviewer and disposition of each issue. |
| Information or book designer / typesetter | After copy lock, when formats and delivery specifications are known. | Page designs with hierarchy, typography, margins, running heads and controlled breaks. Integrate checked charts, maps, illustrations and tables. Supply the commissioned print PDF with printer-required bleed and marks, or digital files such as EPUB or HTML. Verify any required MOBI output against the recipient's specification. |
| Indexer | For non-fiction or reference works after pagination is locked. | An alphabetical analytical index of subjects, people and concepts, with meaningful subentries and cross-references. Return a typeset-ready file and check locators against the final proof. Recheck after repagination and use stable targets for reflowable formats. |
| Proofreader | On the final typeset and indexed proof, after corrections have been applied. | Mark remaining errors in text, headers, captions, links and layout. Compare cover, identifiers including format-specific ISBNs, copyright details, contents and navigation across commissioned formats. Return the proof revision, corrections verified, exceptions and dated pass-for-press recommendation for the authorised human signatory. |
