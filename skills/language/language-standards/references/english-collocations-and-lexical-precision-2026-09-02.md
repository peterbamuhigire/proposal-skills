# English Output Overlay - Proposals

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md) | Canonical study: `C:\wamp64\www\digital-research-skills\docs\continuous-improvement\english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md`

- Default to British English and a neutral East African professional register unless the buyer or submission rules specify otherwise.
- Write for the evaluator's decision: tailored, direct, courteous, evidence-bearing, and calm. Polished language must never conceal a compliance gap or delivery risk.
- Check collocation and grammatical frame before choosing a synonym. Prefer an exact ordinary word over prestige vocabulary; retain the buyer's official wording.
- Calibrate `significant`, `substantial`, `leading`, `robust`, and similar claims to evidence, comparison, mechanism, or qualification.
- Use idioms and figurative language only when the evaluator will understand them and they improve the argument. Literal language wins in ToR compliance, methodology, pricing, risk, and commitments.
- Proof names, dates, figures, prices, cross-references, parallel lists, spelling, and forms. Read the proposal aloud and check every material claim against its proof.

Record buyer, purpose, locale, ToR terms, claim-proof map, collocation and idiom
decisions, language/proof status, unresolved gaps, reviewer, and date.

The supplied books are durable concept inputs, not current procurement, legal,
technical, or donor authorities. Do not reproduce their lists or model answers;
current claims pass Digital Research evaluation and verification.
