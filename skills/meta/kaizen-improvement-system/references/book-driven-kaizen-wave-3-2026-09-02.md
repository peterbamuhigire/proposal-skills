# Book-driven Kaizen Wave 3: proposal application

Use this reference for RFP/RFQ responses, tenders, grants, consulting proposals, and review.

Owning skill: [Kaizen owner](../SKILL.md).

## Transfer controls

- Treat the evaluator journey as a testable information problem: map every requirement to
  compliant evidence, method, owner, measure, assumption, dependency, risk, and acceptance.
- Use a concise story of need, intervention, proof, and beneficiary outcome; never let narrative
  outrun technical, financial, delivery, or source evidence.
- Add evaluator-style red-team checks, failed-path scenarios, data-quality caveats, visual
  readability checks, and a controlled handoff for any AI-assisted drafting.
- Preserve versioned snapshots, decision records, rollback/recovery, and an explicit
  `NOT_ASSESSED` state for unavailable evidence.

## Boundaries

Books are not procurement rules, grant policy, current technology authority, or evidence of
capability. Verify ToR, buyer, legal, platform, security, and financial claims through current
sources. Do not copy exploit procedures, fabricate impact, or imply ranking/AI outcomes.

## Measures and routing

Track compliance coverage, evaluator agreement, evidence/source coverage, unsupported-claim
count, rework, readability/accessibility defects, red-team findings, and submission readiness.
Route to compliance matrix, methodology, M&E, budget, document QA, and currentness skills.

## Currentness gate

Record authority, access date, freshness class, support, uncertainty, and review trigger for
each load-bearing claim before release.
