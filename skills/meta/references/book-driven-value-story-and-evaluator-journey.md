# Book-driven value story and evaluator journey

This reference is an independent synthesis of durable principles from the supplied proposal, storytelling, grant, brand, market, finance, instructional-design, and improvement books.

## Evaluator journey

Design the proposal around the evaluator's decisions: relevance, trust, understanding, feasibility, value, risk, and next action. State the claim, evidence, warrant, assumptions, countercase, owner, and requested decision. Make compliance and scoring traceable rather than relying on persuasive tone.

## Human and outcome story

Use a real beneficiary, customer, or operating context to clarify the problem, stakes, intervention, change, and proof. Keep the story subordinate to truth, consent, evidence, and the evaluation criteria. Never invent a case, outcome, testimonial, partner, or source.

## Delivery system

Make the proposal a controlled operating source for audience, solution, workplan, roles, milestones, readiness, risks, dependencies, feedback, and measures. Link training or enablement to observable behaviour and results. Align budget, capacity, working capital, procurement, and delivery assumptions with the narrative.

## Currentness gate

Verify current laws, procurement rules, funder guidance, standards, prices, technology capabilities, and sector facts through Digital Research and cite the authoritative source at the claim. Mark local book concepts as synthesis and time-sensitive facts with review dates.
