# Institutional Health Communication Proposal Requirements

[Parent skill](../SKILL.md)

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*.

## Proposal evidence

State the health or information problem, affected audiences, evidence base,
uncertainty, engagement model, message design, channels, feedback and correction
path, accessibility and equity controls, qualified review, and outcome measures.
Show how the work moves from one-way dissemination to listening and co-creation
without weakening scientific accuracy. Distinguish reach from comprehension,
trust, and safe action.
