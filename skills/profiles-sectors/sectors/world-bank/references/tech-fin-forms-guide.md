## Current form map

Labels below are concise descriptions, not replacement official forms.

| Form | Content to prepare | Consistency evidence |
|---|---|---|
| TECH-1 | Technical submission and required declarations | Authorised signatory, validity and actual RFP declarations |
| TECH-2 | Consultant organisation and experience, where required | Verified firm records, assignment roles and permitted references |
| TECH-3 | Comments on ToR, counterpart staff and facilities, where required | Clause-specific issue, proposed resolution and explicit assumption |
| TECH-4 | Approach, methodology and work plan | Named activities, deliverables, acceptance and dependencies |
| TECH-5 | Work schedule and planning for deliverables | Delivery dates and review periods agree with TECH-4 and the contract |
| TECH-6 | Team composition, assignment and key experts' inputs; attached CVs | Roles/inputs/CVs agree with method, schedule and remuneration |
| TECH-7 | Code of Conduct for experts | Use required issued form; obtain actual commitments and signatures |
| TECH-8 | Sexual Exploitation and Abuse / Sexual Harassment performance declaration | Verify required declarations; never invent a clean record |
| FIN-1 | Financial submission | Currency, amount, validity and actual signatory authority |
| FIN-2 | Summary of costs | Reconcile remuneration, reimbursables and specified tax treatment |
| FIN-3 | Remuneration breakdown | Inputs reconcile to TECH-6 and the permitted contract basis |
| FIN-4 | Reimbursable expense breakdown | Quantities/rates reconcile to work plan and FIN-2 |

There is no FIN-5 in this inspected checklist. Do not create one by habit.
Additional cost categories belong where the issued financial forms permit
rather than in an invented form. Do not carry forward the older local mapping
of TECH-5 to team composition, TECH-7 to staffing schedule or TECH-8 to Gantt.
The staffing/input schedule is prepared with TECH-6; deliverable planning is
TECH-5. Keep any extra schedule only if the issued RFP permits it.

## Complete the technical response

Start with the issued checklist and transcribe required forms and page limits
into the compliance matrix. A blank template limit is not an unlimited buyer
allowance. Resolve FTP/STP requirements before deciding that a form is optional.

For organisation and experience, select supported assignments relevant to the
actual criteria. State the firm's role, dates and attributable evidence; obtain
consent where personal reference details are included. Do not invent experience,
regional advantage, a minimum project count or universal scoring weights.

For ToR comments, tie each issue to a clause and delivery consequence. A comment
must improve clarity or feasibility without changing required scope by stealth.
For methodology, make each phase produce a named deliverable with an owner,
acceptance test, dependencies and risks. Allocate space against the actual
weighted criteria and caps; no generic percentage decides its importance.

Reconcile TECH-4 activities, TECH-5 schedule and TECH-6 inputs before pricing.
Separate home/field or other categories exactly as requested. CVs require
actual evidence, availability and required certifications/signatures. Never
fill absent identity or qualification evidence with a plausible biography.
TECH-7 and TECH-8 are substantive conduct/declaration obligations, not spare
schedule pages; obtain the appropriate responsible review.

## Complete the financial response

Follow the issued RFP's separate technical/financial submission rules, including
its electronic or physical packaging. Do not assume every portal accepts a
sealed-envelope equivalent or a particular archive format. Technical content
must not leak prices where separation is required.

Derive staff costs from the agreed inputs and supported rates, reconcile totals
across FIN-1 to FIN-4, and state the actual currency and tax basis. Apply the
issued contract's lump-sum/time-based rules. Travel, subsistence, licences,
insurance and other costs require a quantity/rate basis and an allowed category;
do not insert contingencies or taxes that the forms prohibit.

Use the actual evaluation formula for any price/quality sensitivity analysis.
There is no evidenced universal optimum relative to a competitor median, no
assumed technical weighting, and no guarantee that a low price is automatically
rejected. Keep affordability, delivery feasibility and evidence visible.

## Pre-submission verification

Check form presence/order against the issued checklist; signatures and authority;
page counts after rendering; staff/schedule/price reconciliation; required
attachments; filenames/envelope separation; deadline/time zone and submission
receipt procedure. Missing bidder evidence or a mandatory declaration blocks
release even if every structural validator passes. The engine's synthetic
fixture demonstrates the checklist mechanics only.
